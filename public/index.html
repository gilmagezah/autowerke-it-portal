Good to know

Something else on your mind? Send a Consultancy request. It's often free.

What happens after I submit a request?

You'll get an email with your reference number and a link to your private page. We review the request and reply there with next steps, an estimate or a quote.

Will I be charged without agreeing first?

No. Support work starts once you've approved an estimate, projects once you've accepted a fixed price, and paid consultations only after you've agreed the fee.

My issue is urgent. How do I flag it?

On an IT Support request, choose Add priority level and tell us whether you can still work. Higher priorities are looked at first.

Do you work with clients outside Zimbabwe?

Yes. We're based in Harare and most Microsoft 365, Intune and Entra ID work can be done remotely, so we can help wherever you are.

How do I pay?

You'll receive an invoice by email with payment details and a payment reference. A receipt follows once payment is recorded.

How do I check on a request I've already sent?

Use the link in your confirmation email, or track it with your reference number.
