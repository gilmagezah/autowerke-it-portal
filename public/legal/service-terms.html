These terms apply to IT support, projects, domain services and consultancy we provide. Anything specifically agreed in a signed quote, estimate or contract takes priority over these terms.

1. Quotes and scope

  • Quotes and estimates are valid for 14 days unless stated otherwise.

2. What we need from you

  • Timely access to the systems, people and information the work needs, including multi-factor authentication (MFA) codes when required.
  • Accurate information, and someone available to make decisions and approve changes.
  • Current backups of important data before changes, unless backup work is part of our scope.

Delays caused by waiting on access or decisions may affect timelines.

3. Access details and credentials

Logins you share with us are stored encrypted and used only for the agreed work. You stay the owner of your accounts and may change passwords or revoke our access at any time. We delete stored access details within 30 days after the work or management ends.

  • Domains are registered for you, and you remain the owner. We manage them on your behalf and remind you before renewals.

  • When you'd like to move a domain elsewhere, we'll provide what's needed to transfer it.

6. Consultancy

Many consultations are free; free advice is general guidance based on the information you provide. Where a consultation has a fee, we confirm it in writing before charging anything.

7. Sign-off

When work is complete, we send a sign-off document. Signing it (on paper or online) confirms the work was delivered, apart from any outstanding items listed in it.

9. Confidentiality and data protection

We keep your business information confidential. Where our work involves personal information in your systems, we handle it only on your instructions and for the agreed work, with appropriate security, in line with Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act and, where it applies, South Africa's POPIA. See our Privacy Policy.

10. Ending an engagement

Either of us may end ongoing work with written notice. You pay for work done up to that point, and we hand back access and documentation.
