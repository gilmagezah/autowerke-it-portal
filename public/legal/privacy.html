Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026 · Version 2026-10

In short: we collect what we need to do IT work for you (who you are, how to reach you, and what you need), we keep it secure, and we never sell it.

You own your data, systems and domains. We are a service provider, not a data broker.

1. Who we are

AutoWerke | IT ("we", "us") is an IT support and consulting business based in Harare, Zimbabwe. For the personal information described here, we are the data controller under Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07], and the responsible party under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

Data Protection Officer / Information Officer: contact our Data Protection Officer at services@autowerkeit.com.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details: name, company, position, email address and phone number.
  • Request details: what you tell us about your support issue, project, domain or consultancy question, and any screenshots or text files (such as error messages or logs) you attach.
  • Work records: scopes, quotes, time logs, progress updates, sign-offs, invoices and receipts.
  • Signing records: when you sign online, your typed or drawn signature, the time, your IP address and browser, and a fingerprint of the document you signed. This is the evidence that the signature is genuinely yours.
  • Payment documents you choose to upload, such as purchase orders or proof of payment. We do not collect card or bank account numbers.
  • System access details you give us to do the work, such as admin logins or domain registrar accounts. These are encrypted and used only for the work you've asked for (see section 6).
  • Website data: essential cookies that keep secure sessions working, and, only if you agree, Google Analytics (see our Cookie Policy).

4. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the business, whose terms require them to protect it:

  • Supabase: our secure database and private file storage.
  • Render: hosting for this website and portal.
  • Resend: sending our emails to you.
  • Anthropic (Claude): an AI service that reads your request text and any screenshots or text files you attach to help us draft a scope or a first response. A person at AutoWerke | IT always reviews it before anything reaches you. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train their models.
  • Google Analytics: only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. Transfers outside Zimbabwe and South Africa

Some of these providers store or process data outside Zimbabwe and South Africa, for example in the United States or the European Union. Where that happens, we rely on providers that apply strong security (encryption in transit and at rest, access controls) and contractual commitments to protect personal information, as both Zimbabwe's Act and POPIA require for cross-border transfers.

6. How we protect it

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) throughout, and private file storage: documents open only through short-lived, checked links.
  • Passwords and access details you give us are encrypted (AES-256) and only revealed to authorised staff when needed for your work.
  • Our admin systems require a password plus an emailed one-time code, and sessions time out after inactivity.
  • Access is limited to authorised AutoWerke | IT staff who need it for your work.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to: tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy; correct it; delete it, where we don't need to keep it by law; object to or restrict how we use it; and withdraw consent (for example to analytics) at any time. Email services@autowerkeit.com. We'll reply within 30 days.

You can also complain to a regulator: in Zimbabwe, the Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ), acting as the Data Protection Authority; in South Africa, the Information Regulator.

9. If something goes wrong

If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify POTRAZ within 24 hours of becoming aware of it, notify the Information Regulator where South African data is involved, and tell you promptly what happened and what you should do.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell current clients by email.
